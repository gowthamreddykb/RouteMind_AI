## Cleaning decisions

- **Nothing was deleted from the raw file.** Analysis tables are derived copies.
- Orders: 65,752 -> **62,897** after excluding 2,855 `Shipping canceled` orders (4.3%) from lateness analyses, because the dataset defines no late flag for them.
- `Shipping canceled` rows equal `Order Status` in (CANCELED, SUSPECTED_FRAUD): 7,754 of 7,754 rows (verified).
- No imputation was performed. ZIP codes are left missing.
